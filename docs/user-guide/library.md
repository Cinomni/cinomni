# Browsing the library

## Finding your way around

The side bar (a bottom bar on a phone) has the pages every account uses:

| Page | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | *Continue watching* where you left off, *Recently added*, and shortcuts to add or request titles. |
| **Movies** | Every movie you can see, with artwork, year and whether it is available to play. |
| **Series** | The same for series. |
| **Upcoming** | A calendar of episodes and releases that are due. |
| **Requests** | What you asked for and where each request stands. See [Requests](requests.md). |
| **Your account** | Your sign-in security. See [Your account](account.md). |

Administrators also see **Activity** (what is being searched, downloaded and imported) and
**Administration** (the console). See [The console](../administration/console.md).

## Search

Press **Ctrl K** (or use the search button) from anywhere. One box searches:

- titles already in the library, to open them;
- pages of the application, to jump to them;
- and, when you are allowed to, offers to **add** (administrators) or **request** (members) a movie or
  a series by that name.

## A title's page

A movie's page shows its artwork, synopsis, genres and age rating, the files in the library for it
with their resolution, codecs and audio tracks, and a **Play** button once a file is available.
Administrators also see monitoring, the search history and why each release was chosen or rejected.

A series' page lists its seasons and episodes. Each episode shows whether it is available, missing or
not yet aired, and can be played on its own.

## What you can see

What appears in the library can be limited per account:

- **Collections.** An administrator can put titles in collections that only some accounts may open.
- **Content ceiling.** An administrator can set the highest age rating an account may see, for
  example for a child's account.

A title you cannot see does not appear anywhere: not in the lists, not in search, not in Upcoming.
**Your account → What you can watch** tells you whether a limit applies to you. See
[Users and access](../administration/users-and-access.md).
