# Requests

A request is how a household member asks for a movie or a series without being an administrator.

## Asking for a title

If your account may request titles, **Home** offers **Request a movie** and **Request a series**, and
search (**Ctrl K**) offers *Request a movie called …*. Find the title and request it.

Your account may have a limit on how many requests can be **open** at once (pending, or approved and
not yet playable). When you reach it, the page says so; the limit frees up as your requests become
available or are rejected.

## Following a request

**Requests** lists what you asked for:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for an administrator. |
| **Approved** | Cinomni is looking for it or downloading it. |
| **Available** | It is in the library and ready to play. |
| **Rejected** | An administrator declined it, optionally with a reason. |

When a title becomes ready to watch, a notification appears under the bell. A rejection shows its
reason on the **Requests** page.

Some accounts have **Requests skip approval**: their requests are approved the moment they are made.

## For administrators: deciding

**Requests** shows pending requests first, the side bar shows how many are waiting, and each new
request raises an administrator notification. **Approve** adds the title to the catalog so it is
searched for. **Reject** takes an optional
reason that the requester sees. You can filter by status and by who asked.

Who may request, whether their requests need approval, and how many they may have open are set per
account under **Console → Users**; the installation-wide default for open requests is under
**Console → Settings → Requests**. See [Users and access](../administration/users-and-access.md).
